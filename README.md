# 🐾 Pet World

A fun multiplayer clicker game where you click, collect pets, open crates, use potions, merge pets, rebirth, battle other players, and build your collection.

## 🎮 Features

- 🖱️ Click-based gameplay
- 🐾 Collect and equip pets
- 📦 Pet crates
- 🧪 Click and Luck Potions
- 🧬 Merge pets into stronger Shiny pets
- ♻️ Rebirth system
- 🏆 Achievements
- 📊 Player statistics
- 🌎 Multiplayer
- ⚔️ Pet Battles
- 🛡️ Anti-auto-clicker system
- ⚙️ Settings
- 🔐 Player accounts and authentication

## 🕹️ How To Play

1. Open the game.
2. Create an account or log in.
3. Go to the **Game** section.
4. Click the large **CLICK** button.
5. Earn clicks.
6. Use your clicks to open crates and obtain pets.
7. Equip pets to increase your multiplier.
8. Buy potions for temporary boosts.
9. Merge duplicate pets to create stronger Shiny pets.
10. Rebirth when you meet the requirements.
11. Play with other players through multiplayer features.
12. Challenge players in Pet Battles.

## 🐾 Pets

Collect different pets with different multipliers and rarities.

Build your collection and equip your strongest pets to increase your clicking power.

## 📦 Crates

Spend your clicks to open crates and discover new pets.

Different crates can contain different pets and rarities.

## 🧪 Potions

Use temporary boosts to increase your progress.

### Click Potion
Doubles your current base click for 4 minutes.

### Luck Potion
Increases crate luck for a limited time.

## 🧬 Pet Merging

Have two identical pets?

Merge them to create a stronger **Shiny** version.

Each merge costs clicks.

## ♻️ Rebirth

Reset your progress to unlock rebirth bonuses and continue progressing with increased power.

## 🏆 Achievements

Complete different goals while playing and unlock achievements as you progress.

## 🌎 Multiplayer

Pet World includes multiplayer features for interacting with other players.

You can see online players and interact with the multiplayer system.

## ⚔️ Pet Battles

Choose your pet and battle other players.

Build a strong pet collection and use your pets in battles.
